05

Readable by default

Touch & reading48 × 48dp minimum targets, 8dp between adjacent controls. Message text 18sp, supporting 14–16sp. System font scaling; long names and content wrap.
Persian & bidiVazirmatn, leading 1.7. Mirror navigation order, back/disclosure arrows, attachment and send. Don’t mirror phone, search, camera, clock, media or number strings. Isolate +98 numbers, URLs, OTPs and Latin names. Persian digits in chrome; message bodies as sent.
Acceptance gates (not proven by mockups)TalkBack names, roles, order and announcements; keyboard and switch access; 200% font scale; keyboard-open layout; system Back; ی/ي and ک/ك search matching; نیم‌فاصله and mixed-direction selection.

Decisions

R1-01Sub-page app bars have a 48dp back (arrow-right in RTL); ✕ in selection.
R1-02Switch off: outlined track, small thumb.
R1-03Settings rows use a specific icon, never a generic gear.
R1-07Permanent actions use error-container at 48dp.
R1-08Undo snackbars sit at the bottom, above navigation or composer.
R1-09One strong action per screen; escapes use Quiet.
R1-11Text on lilac uses on-secondary-container-variant (≥ 4.5:1).
R2-15Conversation previews are one line with an ellipsis.
R2-19Search matches are bold on a lilac highlight.
L5Dark inbox: roles from tokens, avatars keep light identity colors.
L9Empty state per filter; the FAB always shows.
N1–N5Coral Library badge (99+) is the spam entry point. Chips: All, Unread, People, Services, Unknown — scrolling.
