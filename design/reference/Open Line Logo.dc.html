OPEN LINE · LOGO · TURN 3

Typing bubble, six ways

Variations on 2c, with different bubble shapes and palettes that go beyond the design system. The first dot always stands out; at 28px all three dots turn the same colour so they stay readable.

3aPill · Tomato

A wide pill bubble, warm and friendly. A cream bubble on tomato red, with a sunflower dot.

3bCircle · Electric

A round bubble with a sharp little tail. Electric blue with a hot-pink first dot; bold and very readable.

3cSquircle · Night Lime

A soft square with the tail in the centre. A black bubble on lime, like a sticker; it stands out on any wallpaper.

3dCorner · Violet

The square-corner bubble from 1b, now with typing dots. Violet with a mint dot, a little more techy.

3eOutline · Ember

An outlined bubble on near-black, with an orange ember dot. Quiet and premium; best on dark home screens.

3fClassic · Sunset

The 2c shape on a soft coral-to-rose blend. Warm and personal; the gradient is the only one in the set.
