# Messages — Open Line

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** Personal messaging as a clear, living line between people. The inbox favors sender, latest meaning, and urgency over decorative conversation cards.

**OWN WORLD:** Cool mineral paper, deep pine ink, sharp lime for new activity, lilac for media, coral for attention. A fine route line, round contact markers, flat slabs, and strong typography replace the current cobalt styling. No literal vintage hardware.

**STORY:** See who needs attention, open a conversation, reply with confidence, and recover or quiet unwanted traffic.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A dark identity band holds Messages and search. A compact attention count leads into a white continuous list. Unread entries carry lime signal marks. Compose stays within thumb reach.

**FORM:** A modernized telephone switchboard. Grounded direction 3 from Impeccable seed `805d6c2b`; its line grammar maps to inbox, thread, composer, settings, and states.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

## Scope

Operate mode. Pendev concept covering core journeys, settings, dialogs, empty and error states, dark and RTL variants, and reusable components. Illustrative people and message text are fictional. This is design work, not an Android implementation.
