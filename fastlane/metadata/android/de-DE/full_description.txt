Messages is a private, lightweight SMS/MMS client for Android.

📱 STAY CONNECTED WITH EASE:
Send SMS and MMS, use group messaging, and share photos, emojis, and attachments.

🚫 BLOCK UNWANTED MESSAGES:
Block numbers and keywords, export and import block lists, and filter obvious spam into a separate folder.

🔒 SMS BACKUP:
Export and import messages so you can move between devices without losing conversations.

🚀 LIGHTWEIGHT:
Small download size with fast startup and efficient inbox updates.

🔐 ENHANCED PRIVACY:
Choose what appears on the lock screen. No ads and no unnecessary permissions.

🔍 EFFICIENT SEARCH:
Find conversations and messages quickly without endless scrolling.

🌈 MODERN DESIGN:
Clean Material interface with light and dark themes and customizable colors.
